# Claim911 demo: one family, one connected workflow

Duration: 3:58, 1080p, English.

## Story spine

Priya’s family member is medically ready for discharge. She needs to know whether they can leave and what they still owe. Every scene either identifies a blocker, removes one, obtains permission, verifies a receiving-side result or explains the financial outcome.

The four parts remain visible throughout: find the blocker, collect the evidence, approve and submit, verify the outcome.
