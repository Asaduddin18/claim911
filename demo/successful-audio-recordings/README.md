# Successful demo audio recordings

These are the finalized, archived conversations belonging to the completed 13-checkpoint synthetic discharge case. Bolna is the actual assistant. All counterparties and institution records are synthetic.

There are 11 voice stages and two non-call checkpoints (exact packet approval and independent receipt verification). Stage 5 includes two successful conversations that correctly reported a pending signature, followed by the successful signed-summary follow-up. Those pending outcomes demonstrate the recovery path; they are not failed call attempts.

The audio joins captured speech turns with short pauses. Waiting time is compressed. These are recorded rehearsals, not a fresh uninterrupted live call sequence.
